How we work

One dedicated team from first meeting to final pack-down — so you can be a guest at your own event.

Consult

We listen first — your story, guests, venue, culture and budget — and shape a clear brief together.

Design

Mood boards, 3D concepts, florals, lighting and stationery, refined until every detail feels like you.

Build

Our in-house crew constructs the stage, kosha, runway and installations on site, overnight if needed.

Celebrate

We run the day — guests, hospitality, timings and teardown — while you enjoy every moment.
